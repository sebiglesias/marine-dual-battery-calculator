Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Automatic Charging Relay (ACR) on a boat?
An ACR connects your starting battery and house battery together automatically whenever the outboard alternator is charging (over 13.0V). When the engine is turned off and voltage drops, the ACR immediately disconnects the two banks, preventing stereos and chartplotters from ever draining your engine starting battery.
Why can't I use a standard deep-cycle battery to start my outboard engine?
Deep-cycle batteries have thick lead plates designed for slow, continuous power over many hours. Starting an outboard engine requires an instantaneous surge of 500 to 1,000 cold cranking amps. Using a deep cycle battery for starting causes severe voltage sag and can damage sensitive outboard starter motors.
Why is marine tinned copper wire required over standard automotive wire?
Automotive wire uses bare copper strands. In salty humid marine air, moisture wicks beneath the insulation, turning the copper green with verdigris corrosion within months. ABYC compliant marine wire has individually electro-tinned copper strands that resist salt oxidation indefinitely.
